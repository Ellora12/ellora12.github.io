Ellora Yasi

Hello! My name is Ellora, and I have graduated from Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST) with a degree in Computer Science Engineering in April 2024. I am passionate about coding, problem-solving, research, and continuous learning. Currently, I work as a Software Engineer at WellDev, where I contribute to developing software solutions and improving user experiences. I also interned at Trust Bank Limited, where I gained hands-on experience in software development, security, and vulnerability assessment.

I worked as a Research Assistant at MIST after graduation, focusing on machine learning, deep learning, image processing, and model deployment. My research focuses on applying AI and machine learning to real-world problems, particularly in flood prediction and analysis, agricultural AI, computer vision, explainable AI, and privacy-preserving machine learning. My work includes rice leaf disease classification, air-pollution-associated disease burden prediction, food waste reduction, and food equity. My undergraduate thesis focused on flood prediction in Bangladesh using both tabular and image data. You can view a demo of the project.

During university, I have been actively involved in programming competitions, hackathons, and technical activities. I was an active member of the MIST Computer Club and served as the Executive Director of the Competitive Programming Committee during my final year. I participated in national-level programming contests such as IUPC, NCPC and NGPC, experiences that strengthened my problem-solving and critical-thinking skills while allowing me to mentor and inspire juniors. I also served as Vice President of Content Writing and Promotions at the MIST Cyber Security Club, where I helped organize flag-hunting competitions and technical seminars.

When I’m away from my laptop, I enjoy playing Rubik's Cube, chess,and badminton. I have also been part of my department's cricket and football tournaments. I enjoy travelling, exploring new places, and discovering different cuisines.
