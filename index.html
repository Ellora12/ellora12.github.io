Ellora Yasi

Hello! My name is Ellora, and I completed my graduation in Computer Science Engineering from Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST) in April 2024. I am passionate about research, coding, problem-solving, and learning new things.

I have been working as a Research Assistant with Dr. Najmul Hasan at BRAC University alongside my professional role, contributing to MealMates, an intelligent platform for food equity and waste reduction. Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant with Dr. Md. Mahbubur Rahman and Dr. Nusrat Sharmin at MIST after graduation, focusing on machine learning, deep learning, image processing, and model deployment. My research focuses on applying AI to real-world challenges in flood prediction and analysis, agricultural AI, explainable AI, privacy-preserving machine learning, and food systems. My work spans flood forecasting and segmentation, rice leaf disease classification, air-pollution-associated disease burden prediction, smart agriculture, food waste reduction, and food equity. My undergraduate thesis focused on flood prediction in Bangladesh, combining tabular meteorological data and image-based analysis.

Currently, I work as a Junior Software Engineer at WellDev, working with the InnoTix team on backend development and maintenance of a large multi-tenant platform serving 42+ European public transportation clients, while resolving issues and improving reliability, security, and user experience. I also interned at Trust Bank Limited, where I gained hands-on experience in software development, security, and vulnerability assessment.

During university, I was actively involved in programming competitions, hackathons, and innovative idea presentation competitions. I was an active member of the MIST Computer Club and served as the Executive Director of the Competitive Programming Committee during my final year. I participated in national-level programming contests such as IUPC, NCPC, and NGPC, experiences that strengthened my problem-solving and critical-thinking skills while allowing me to mentor and inspire juniors. I also served as Vice President of Content Writing and Promotions at the MIST Cyber Security Club, where I helped organize flag-hunting competitions and technical seminars.

When I’m away from my laptop, I enjoy quiet walks in green spaces. I also like playing Rubik’s Cube, chess, and badminton, and have participated in my department’s cricket and football tournaments. Outside of these activities, I enjoy travelling and experiencing new places and cultures.
